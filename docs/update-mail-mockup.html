5 · Importing a reply

  
The rules, the same for clicked and typed replies:

  
· Reference (leave as is): lists the tasks in order, so 2 is the second one. It comes back in the quoted original even when they write at the top.

    · A line 2 done closes task 2 (in an ask). 2: words or re 2 words is a comment on it, or the answer if it is a question.

    · Words with no number go to the task in the footer line of a clicked reply. ok alone is Got it for every task.

    · Anything left over goes to a box in the review, where you pick the task.
