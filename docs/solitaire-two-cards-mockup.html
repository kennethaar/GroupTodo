How to play · why it is strict GTD ▾
  • Laid out like Klondike. Projects to review lie face down in the deck, top left. The project you are reviewing is the card turned up next to it. It shows what the project says now: its outcome and its next action. The five piles are on the right. Every card on the table is the same size.
  • One question at a time, one card per answer. The answers are dealt below the piles in three overlapping rows, in the same places as the numpad keys, with 0 and . underneath. Aim at a card and it is pulled up so you can read it. Play it and it lands on the project.
  • Every project is played to one of five piles: Active ♠, Waiting for ♣, Someday/Maybe ♦, Done ♥ or Cancelled ✕. This is the weekly “am I still committed?” check. The 5 card is always “as it is”. The height of a pile, or of the deck, shows how much is in it. There are no numbers to count.
  • An active project is always asked about its next action. The current one lies on 5, so Enter keeps it. The other open tasks lie around it, in case one of them is really the next action. The . card is for a next action you have not captured yet. Just start typing.
  • Otherwise only the gaps are asked: a missing outcome, a context for a new next action, and who a waiting project waits on. Anything already set is not asked again. You choose a person only when the project is waiting.
  • Letting go counts. A cancelled or parked project lands on its pile like any other card. A shorter list is a win.
  • The streak. Finish the deck to keep your weekly streak, then beat your best time. Afterwards there is a bonus round for Someday/Maybe.
