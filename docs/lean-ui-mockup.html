Help

GroupTodo keeps your tasks, projects and notes as plain files in folders you already own. Everything you see here is read from those files, and everything you change is written straight back to them.

Getting around

  • The sidebar lists the places you go every day. Hide it with ⇤ at its top, or press \. Bring it back with ☰.
  • Your page opens when you click GroupTodo at the top left. Set your picture there, keep notes about yourself, and see everything in every space that links to you.
  • Back and forward work everywhere, including after you open a full page. Use ← and → at the top, or Alt+← and Alt+→.
  • The capture box adds a task from anywhere. Press N to jump to it. Write @name for a person, @@place for a context, #project for a project and a day like friday for the due date.
  • Search finds tasks, pages, people and commands such as Raw text.

Spaces

  • Each space is its own folder, shared with the people in it. The squares at the top right show which spaces are on.
  • Tick any combination. Every view then shows exactly those spaces, and each item carries a coloured tag for its space while more than one is showing.
  • Every page always says which space it lives in.

Today

  • At the top is what is due today or late, then the next seven days, then your journal for each space, such as Your Work journal.
  • Click a section's title or its arrow to fold it away. Click again to open it.
  • Click the bullet in front of a section, or in front of any line, to focus on it. Everything else leaves the screen and only the trail above it stays. Click a step in the trail to go back out.
  • On a wide screen the journals sit side by side, one column per space.
  • History, next to the date, scrolls back through everything that happened, day by day. Dismissing “Since you were last here” loses nothing.

Writing on a line

  • On Today and on every page, click a line and type. A menu opens right under the line to set its state, due date, context, person, project and repeat. Each item shows its key.
  • Typing @, @@ or # opens people, contexts or projects and filters them as you type.
  • When a line becomes Waiting, the menu asks who you are waiting on. Waiting is always on somebody else.
  • Tab and Shift+Tab indent and outdent. The arrow on the left of a line folds its children.

Projects

  • A project's status is the folder its file sits in: _ Cancelled, 1 Active, 2 Someday/Maybe, 3 Waiting for and 0 Completed. Drag a card on the board to move it, and every link to it follows.
  • A project whose open next actions are all waiting on somebody shows as Waiting for, marked “auto”.
  • The timeline shows the plan ahead. ▸ unfolds a project into its next actions, and dragging a bar reschedules it.
  • The report shows what got done. Pick a week, month, quarter, half year, year or your own dates from the list, then Copy as text for a status mail.

People and organisations

  • On the People board, drop a person on a project to involve them, or on a next action to hand it over. It then shows in your Waiting for. Drop yourself on it to take it back.
  • Organisations work the same way, on projects only.
  • People outside the space get a check-in sheet when you ask them for an update.

Pages

  • Clicking a page opens a preview with its properties, notes and links. Open full page shows everything, with each linked reference quoted in context.
  • Write [[a title]] to link to a page. A dashed link points to a page nobody has made yet. Click it to make it.
  • Pages has four views: List, Board, Mind map and Graph.

Weekly review

  • Five steps, about fifteen minutes. The projects step is played as solitaire: one project at a time, stalest first.
  • Every field shows its options, taken from the project's own space. Click one, or drag the card onto it. Add a missing area or organisation straight from the field.
