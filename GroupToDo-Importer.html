GroupToDo Importer

Turns a Logseq or Obsidian vault into a GroupToDo folder. Your old vault is only read, never changed: everything is written into a new, empty folder you choose at the end.

1Your old vault

The folder Logseq or Obsidian opens: for Logseq the one with journals and pages in it, for Obsidian the one with .obsidian.

2You

Day pages in GroupToDo belong to a person, so your journal goes into a folder with your name.

3What each kind of page becomes

In GroupToDo the folder a page sits in says what it is: a person, a project, a context. Say what your folders, namespaces and tags mean. A tag wins over a folder. Anything left as Plain page comes in under its own name.

4Test the conversion

Nothing is written. You see what would happen, what could not be carried over, and any page side by side, before and after.

5Write the new vault

Choose an empty folder. Everything is written there; the old vault stays as it is.
